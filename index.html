Galen Cassidy Peria, also known as Duke Aeroplane, is a song-finder, wordsmith, filmmaker, student of the cards, histrionic, and excitable drinker. He is based in New Orleans, Louisiana, where he makes a highly-regarded crawfish étouffée, plays piano for his cat, lights candles to St. Jude, reads mouldering paperback novels, and screams at televised baseball. The Duke hopes you will enjoy his website and then get the hell off the internet.
