---
title: The Man In The Moon
category: lyrics
album: 'New Orleans Night: Live in New Orleans'
---


the man in the moon
with his lantern askance
throws sparks that catch in the trees
he strikes up a tune
and the stars start to dance
and carry on for as long as they please
the days come and gone
had somewhere to go
somewhere that they had to be
they steal ‘cross the lawn
with lamps burning low
the shadows are something to see
to drift with the saints
barefoot in the grass
or whistle all night in the dark
it’s only grace
dancing the last
turned like an offhand remark
