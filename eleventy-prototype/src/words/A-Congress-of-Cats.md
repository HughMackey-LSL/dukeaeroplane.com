---
title: A Congress of Cats
category: poetry
---



there is a congress of cats on the block tonight

out among the garbage cans and potted palms

the chipped ramshackle patchwork-pavement

and detritus of last night’s poker game

they seem to be looking to me

for answers i cannot provide

and i am comforted by the questions

they are kind enough not to ask

i am smoking a slim cuban cigar

and wondering how long it will be

before i am again somewhere lacking in these

inconvenient embargoes

somewhere rum is cheaper than milk

i am wondering how many of our respective 9 lives are spent

and who among us holds rank

and i am doubting - rather gravely - that it is me

seems there is a dog causing problems half a block down

the noise is met with cautious derision by my feline compatriots

we all nod in agreed understanding

that dogs are always causing problems

it seems there is some rain coming in from the gulf

and this is where we split hairs, the cats and i

because i welcome it

welcome the soft tropical breeze

that rustles the branches of trees

and touches these vespertine shadows

with some sort of anticipation

now the crazed barking intensifies

and the cats scatter

the cigar is spent

and the ice in my drink is melted

there is a siren from down by Claiborne

and something that sounds like a snare drum

yes, something is en route for sure

if not a storm blown clear across from Africa

maybe just a train from Chicago

or a time-tested ballad on WWOZ

maybe some kind of notice in the mails

or better yet, a full page public service announcement in the Picayune

repealing rent, hunger, fear, and war

something to the effect of:

attention all creatures great and small

don’t you weep no more



*-April 4, 2022*

N*ew Orleans, LA*
