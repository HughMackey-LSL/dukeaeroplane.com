The mail carrier grumbled, but the box made it: a fresh pressing of *Higher
Ground* on heavyweight wax, and it sounds like a Sunday morning after a long
Saturday night.

There is no fancy store here. If you want one, the arrangement is simple — you
[drop the Duke a line](contact.html), and a record makes its way to you. That is
the whole operation.

Supplies are, as they say, limited. Mostly because the man himself keeps giving
them away to anyone who compliments his hat.
