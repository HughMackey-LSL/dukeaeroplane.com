Well, here I am again — somewhere north of nowhere, a cup of gas-station coffee
going cold on the dashboard and a notebook full of half-finished verses on the
passenger seat.

The tour has been good to me. The rooms have been small and the crowds smaller,
which is exactly how I like it. There is nothing quite like playing a song for
eleven strangers and a bartender who has heard it all before.

## What's coming

A few new songs have been clawing their way out of me. I do not want to promise
anything — promises and I have a difficult relationship — but there may be a
recording before the year is out.

Until then, keep the devil in the hole and the road in front of you.
